# Blitzkrieg

**Blitzkrieg** turns the Blitzkrieg training method into an automatic
practice tracker for Geometry Dash.

No spreadsheets. No manual run tracking.

Create a profile, play the level, and let the mod track your
**<cg>runs, stages, attempts, playtime and progress</c>** automatically.

# Quick Start

1. Create or find a level copy with **<cy>start positions</c>**
   *(around 12-18 is recommended)*.
2. Open Blitzkrieg and **<cg>create a profile</c>**.
3. Your training stages will be generated automatically.
4. Start playing.

That's it — Blitzkrieg handles the tracking in the background.

You can also connect the same profile to **multiple levels**, such as
a startpos copy and the original level.

# What Blitzkrieg Tracks

- **<cg>Runs</c>** — automatically detected during gameplay
- **<cg>Attempts</c>** — per run and per stage
- **<cg>Playtime</c>** — time spent practicing
- **<cg>Best Run</c>** — your best progress for each range
- **<cg>First Completion</c>** — attempts and progress from your first clear
- **<cg>Completions</c>** — repeated successful runs
- **<cg>Stage Progress</c>** — overall training progression

# More Tools

- **<cg>Stage Browser</c>** — inspect completed and upcoming stages
- **<cg>Stage Graph</c>** — visualize attempts and playtime
- **<cg>Multi-Level Profiles</c>** — track several level copies together
- **<cg>Noclip Detection</c>** — invalid runs can be ignored automatically
- **<cg>Custom Notifications</c>** — feedback for completed runs and stages
- **<cg>Startpos Copies</c>** — use curated practice copies directly from the mod

# Recommended Setup

For the most reliable tracking, we recommend using **2.1% percentages**
while practicing Blitzkrieg.

<mod:zsa.percentage-toggle>
